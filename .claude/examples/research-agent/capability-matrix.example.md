## Why they likely did it that way

- **TeamFlo — no approval step:** optimizes for fast, viral team growth in
  small teams; trades some invite noise for maximum signup velocity.
- **TeamFlo — real-time notification:** billing-sensitive small-team
  buyers likely make instant seat visibility a trust/retention feature,
  not a security control.
- **Huddleworks — widened approval:** looks like a direct answer to the
  same single-admin bottleneck behind our own idea's origin — widen who
  can approve rather than remove the gate entirely.
- **Huddleworks — flat billing:** avoids needing to notify anyone about
  individual seat additions by pricing structure, likely to reduce billing
  complexity and support load rather than as a considered visibility
  tradeoff.
- **RosterPilot — threshold-based approval:** a cheap heuristic
  guardrail — no friction for the common small-team case, with a
  size-based catch for runaway growth.
- **RosterPilot — ambient banner:** shifts from per-event notification to
  always-visible awareness, plausibly to avoid notification fatigue for
  larger, faster-growing teams.
