### `decision-2026-08-20-restrict-invite-to-full-members`

**Status:** active · **Confidence:** high

**Recommendation:** Self-invite is available to full members only;
guests and contractors are excluded.

**Alternatives considered:**
1. *Allow every role (including guests and contractors) to invite* —
   cons: widens the risk surface beyond what's been examined; contractors/
   guests may not be accountable for who they add. Rejected because: the
   requester explicitly excluded these roles when asked directly.
2. *Allow only the full-member role to invite* — pros: matches
   requester's stated intent; keeps the risk surface to accountable,
   permanent teammates. *(chosen)*

**Rationale:** Requester confirmed directly that "any team member" was
never meant to include guests or contractors — narrowing scope to full
members before any further design work avoids designing against a
broader, unintended blast radius.

**Open questions:** Are there full-member sub-roles (e.g. read-only) that
should also be excluded? Not yet asked.

**Status:** active · **Confidence:** medium

**Recommendation:** Notify the budget owner via a weekly digest

**Alternatives considered:**

4. *Persistent ambient seat-count and cost banner* — always current;
   follows RosterPilot's approach. Rejected because the stated preference

## 20. Functional Requirements

All four requirements elaborate `decision-2026-09-02-budget-owner-notification-mechanism`:

- **`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-content-and-trigger`** — The system
  sends a weekly digest email to the budget owner listing every seat
  added via self-invite in the preceding 7 days.
- **`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-delivery-channel`** — The weekly
  digest is delivered to the budget owner via email, not an in-app
  notification or banner. *(This resolves the decision's own
  "email vs. in-app" open question — resolved inline as a small
  implementation choice, per `solution-design`'s own rule against
  spinning up a new decision record for it.)*
- **`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-cadence`** — The digest cadence is
  fixed at 7 days, aligned to a consistent day of the week, not a rolling
  window recalculated from account creation.
- **`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-running-total-and-cost`** — Digest
  content includes a running total seat count and current estimated
  monthly cost impact, in addition to the list of newly added seats.

## 21. Acceptance Criteria

Grouped by requirement (all elaborate
`decision-2026-09-02-budget-owner-notification-mechanism`):

**`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-content-and-trigger`:**
- If at least one seat was added in the preceding 7 days, exactly one
  digest email is sent on the scheduled digest day.
- If zero seats were added in the preceding 7 days, no digest email is
  sent for that period.
- Each seat listed includes the date added and who added it, if
  available.

**`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-delivery-channel`:**
- The digest arrives in the budget owner's account email inbox, not
  solely as an in-app notification.
- No equivalent content is duplicated as a persistent in-app banner or
  notification badge.

**`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-cadence`:**
- Digest emails for a given account are always sent on the same day of
  the week.
- The reporting window covers exactly the 7 days since the previous
  digest, with no gap and no overlap.

**`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-running-total-and-cost`:**
- The digest displays the total seat count as of generation time.
- The digest displays the estimated monthly cost at the current seat
  count.

## 22. Edge Cases

Grouped by requirement. **Partial: 3 of 4 requirements specify edge
cases; `requirement-2026-09-05-digest-running-total-and-cost` does not.**

- **`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-content-and-trigger`:** if more seats
  are added between digest generation and delivery (e.g. a send failure
  and retry), the digest covers seats added up through generation time,
  not delivery time, to avoid double-counting on retry.
- **`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-delivery-channel`:** if the budget
  owner has no verified email address on file, the digest fails loudly
  rather than silently never sending.
- **`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-cadence`:** explicitly names
  `risk-2026-09-02-digest-misses-burst-spend` — if many seats are added
  in a short burst within a single window, they all appear together in
  the same digest; this requirement does not add burst detection or an
  earlier trigger.
- **`requirement-2026-09-05-digest-running-total-and-cost`:** no edge
  cases specified in the record.

**`monitoring`** (severity: high) — `system-impact-2026-09-10-monitoring-silent-failure`:
